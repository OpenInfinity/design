标志Logo

两片按黄金比例（1 : 0.618）缩放的"叶"，以直角在 45° 对角线上相接，构成向右下延伸的无穷符号。字标为 Geist Medium，已转为曲线。Two leaves in the golden ratio (1 : 0.618) meet corner to corner on a 45° diagonal, forming an infinity sign that extends down and to the right. The wordmark is Geist Medium, converted to outlines.

横版组合 · 首选Horizontal lockup · primary

标志与字标间距 1.5X，字标字号为标志高度的 0.7 倍。1.5X between mark and wordmark; wordmark size is 0.7 × mark height.

竖版组合Vertical lockup

用于方形或居中版面，标志与字标间距 1.25X。For square or centred layouts; 1.25X between mark and wordmark.

图形标志Mark

应用图标、头像、水印，或空间不足时使用。For app icons, avatars, watermarks and tight spaces.

文字标志Wordmark

同一画面已出现图形标志时使用。Use when the mark already appears nearby.

构造与安全区CONSTRUCTION & CLEAR SPACE
  • 比例Proportion大叶 : 小叶 = 1 : 0.618。大叶在左上，小叶在右下，不可翻转。Big leaf : small leaf = 1 : 0.618. Big leaf top-left, small leaf bottom-right; never mirrored.
  • 单位 XUnit XX = 小叶半径 = 0.191 × 标志宽度。所有间距以 X 为单位。X = small-leaf radius = 0.191 × mark width. All spacing uses X.
  • 安全区Clear space标志四周至少留 1X（虚线框），框内不放文字、图形或画面边缘。Keep at least 1X on every side (dashed box), free of text, graphics and edges.
  • 最小尺寸Minimum size屏幕：图形 16 px，横版组合 96 px 宽。印刷：图形 6 mm，横版组合 25 mm 宽。Screen: mark 16 px, horizontal lockup 96 px wide. Print: mark 6 mm, horizontal lockup 25 mm wide.

应用图标App icons

标志宽度约为图标宽度的 53%，居中放置。交付稿为方形、无圆角，圆角和玻璃效果由各平台系统添加。The mark spans about 53% of the icon width, centred. Deliverables are square with no rounded corners — each platform applies its own mask and effects.

iOS / iPadOS

Xcode 资源目录：一份 1024 源图，含默认、深色、着色三种外观，直接替换工程里的 AppIcon.appiconset。Xcode asset catalog: one 1024 source with default, dark and tinted appearances. Replace the project's AppIcon.appiconset.

macOS

macOS 26 起用 Icon Composer 分层（见下方说明）；macOS 11–15 用 .icns 或资源目录，按 824 网格制作。From macOS 26 use the Icon Composer layers (guide below); for macOS 11–15 use the .icns or asset catalog built on the 824 grid.

Icon Composer

macOS 26 / iOS 26 的 .icon 分层素材：前景层为 1024 透明画布，背景层设为纯色 #FFFFFF，深色外观为 #000000。Layers for macOS 26 / iOS 26 .icon files: a 1024 transparent foreground; set the background to #FFFFFF, or #000000 in dark appearance.

色彩Color


墨与白是主色。石墨、雾白、午夜用于界面与深色场景。信号橙、电光蓝、青柠是可选强调色，建议只选一个长期使用。点击色值即可复制。CMYK 为近似换算，印刷前请打样校色。Ink and Paper are primary. Graphite, Mist and Midnight serve UI and dark contexts. Signal, Electric and Lime are optional accents — pick one and keep it. Click a value to copy it. CMYK values are approximations; proof before printing.

动效Motion


所有动作以两叶相接的点为原点，时长按黄金比递减：600 / 370 / 230 ms。系统开启"减少动态效果"时显示静态标志。视频提供白底、黑底和透明背景三种。Every movement pivots on the point where the leaves meet. Durations step down by the golden ratio: 600 / 370 / 230 ms. With reduced motion enabled, show the static mark. Videos come on white, black and transparent backgrounds.










开场 RevealReveal
大叶 600 ms 从相接点长出，小叶延迟 230 ms、用 370 ms 长出，字标 370 ms 淡入。曲线 cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)。用于启动页和视频片头。Big leaf grows from the contact point in 600 ms; small leaf follows after 230 ms over 370 ms; wordmark fades in over 370 ms. Curve cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). For launch screens and video intros.








无限 InfiniteInfinite
两叶以相接点为轴交替放大缩小（1 ↔ 0.618），2.4 s 一个来回，可无缝循环。曲线 cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)。用于等待、思考中和空状态。The leaves swap size around the contact point (1 ↔ 0.618), 2.4 s per cycle, seamless loop. Curve cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1). For waiting, thinking and empty states.








加载 LoadingLoading
标志绕相接点每次转 90°，每步 600 ms 缓入缓出。用于加载和同步中的指示器，最小 16 px。The mark turns 90° at a time around the contact point, 600 ms per step with ease-in-out. For loading and syncing indicators, 16 px minimum.

使用规范Usage


标志只能等比缩放，方向、比例和颜色关系都不能改动。拿不准时，请使用黑或白的原始文件。Only scale the logo proportionally; never change its orientation, proportions or colours. When in doubt, use the original black or white files.






✓ 等比缩放Scale proportionally


✕ 不要拉伸变形Don't stretch


✕ 不要旋转（45° 斜向是标志的一部分）Don't rotate (the 45° diagonal is part of the mark)


✕ 不要改变 1 : 0.618 的比例Don't change the 1 : 0.618 ratio


✕ 不要加阴影、渐变或立体效果Don't add shadows, gradients or 3D effects


✕ 两片叶不要用不同颜色Don't colour the leaves differently


✕ 不要放在低对比度的底色上Don't place on low-contrast backgrounds


✕ 不要改成描边Don't outline the mark
